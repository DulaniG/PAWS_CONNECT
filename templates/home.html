🐾 PAWS CONNECT

Street Animal Rescue and Adoption System in Sri Lanka

Report • Rescue • Care • Adopt

PAWS CONNECT is a mobile-friendly web platform that helps the public, rescuers, and shelters coordinate street animal reporting, rescue, treatment tracking, and adoption in Sri Lanka.

Get Started Login

📍

Animal Reporting

Public users can report injured, abandoned, or vulnerable street animals with details, photos, and location information.

🚑

Rescue Coordination

Shelters can review reports, prioritize rescue cases, and assign rescuers based on urgency and location.

🏥

Treatment Tracking

Shelters can update animal treatment progress, recovery status, and adoption availability.

🏡

Adoption Management

Users can browse rescued animals and submit adoption requests through a structured adoption workflow.

Who Can Use PAWS CONNECT?

👤 Public User

Reports animals and submits adoption requests.

🚨 Rescuer

Responds to assigned rescue cases and updates rescue status.

🏠 Shelter

Reviews reports, manages animals, and processes adoptions.
